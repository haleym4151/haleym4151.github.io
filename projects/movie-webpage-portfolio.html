Movie Webpage Portfolio

Context

With a partner, we were required to make a movie web project about makeing your own movie and or a part 2 of an already exsisting movie that would allow us to practice our HTML and CSS concepts. So, me & my partner ｄecided to do a yuri (lesbian) romance, vocaloid (a singing voice synthesizer software) & drama movie.

Content

We had to apply our knowledge of HTML & CSS we have leaned in order to develop a webpage.

Some requirements for HTML and CSS were:

HTML

  • Headings
    • Paragraphs
  • Div's
  • Inputs
  • Submit button
  • Customized Google fonts

CSS

  • Background colors
  • Margins
  • Paddings<>
  • Borders
  • CSS selectors such as element / class / id
  • At least two local images

Additionally, he offered the opportunity to earn extra credit by assigning us the task to make a sequel to our initial story using the same CSS. Me & my partner took the offer & created a sequel called "Mesmerizing Endroll: The return of the yellow one".

To sumarize the story, it's about three vocaloids, Akita Neru-Villan, who is jelous of Teto being with Miku, Kasane Teto-lover of Miku & Hastune Miku-lover of Teto. With Akita Neru wanting to mezmorize Miku into being with her instead of Kasane Teto, to only get defeated by Teto & end up being "friends" with them to only get revenge on them later on.

Reflection

Some challenges me & my partner faced while making this project was trying to understand & how to use padding & margin for our images to be in the right size. From this challenges, I was able to learn how to overcome it again if I ever need to use padding & margin to size my images in feuture projects. In the beginning me & my partnern had no ideas of what type of story or movie to come up with since we both had drastically different taste in shows & movie taste, but we did find that we both like vocaloid & some type of romance genre we decited to stick with it instead of pondering on the story idea for too long. A takeaway from this is to get your priorities straight when it comes to assignments.
